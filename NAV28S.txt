NAV for the HP-28S — keying listing
============================================================

Setup: make a directory first:  'NAV' CRDIR  then press NAV in the USER menu.
For every entry below, type the object exactly as shown, then type the name
in quotes and STO.  Example:   69.2  'DT' STO
Programs can be keyed in any order. Line breaks and indentation are only for
readability; spaces between objects are what matter.

Inputs and outputs are the same as on the HP-48 version, except that results
are plain numbers on the stack (no tags), in the same order.
Angles are decimal degrees, N and E positive. UT is entered as H.MMSS.
Valid for 2024-2050 (the planet and Sun theory is fitted to that window).

PLOT draws the lines of position with PIXEL, with the DR at the axis origin
and the fix marked by a small cross. Press any key to return; the fix is then
left on the stack. Scale: the screen's half-height is shown nowhere on this
model, so read positions from the stack.

After keying each data table, verify it with CHK: put the table on the stack
(e.g. press STARS), then CHK. You get one number per row (row 1 highest on the
stack, last row at level 1). Compare them with the checksum list for that table.
A typo too small to change the checksum at 10 digits is too small to matter.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. SETTINGS
------------------------------------------------------------

69.2  'DT' STO

0  'IC' STO

2  'HE' STO

10  'TEMP' STO

1010  'PRES' STO

DT   = Delta T (TT - UT) in seconds
IC   = index correction in arcminutes, sign as applied (negative if 'on the arc')
HE   = height of eye in metres
TEMP = air temperature, deg C;  PRES = pressure, hPa
